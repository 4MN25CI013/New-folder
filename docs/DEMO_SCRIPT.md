# Three-minute demo script

1. **Set expectations (20 seconds).** Show the `SYNTHETIC` badge, the latest data timestamp and cleaner counters. Read the banner aloud: “Advisory only — read-only — no authentication, not production-secure.” Explain that the values are generated and the view is not connected to facility controls.
2. **Spot a capacity risk (35 seconds).** In Operator view, point to the sorted alert cards and choose a high-risk resource such as `VM-R3-01` or `STO-R1-01`. Compare current utilisation against the safety threshold and the separate hard limit. Explain that an exhaustion date is withheld unless positive growth passes the significance gate.
3. **Explain the forecast (45 seconds).** Open the resource detail. Walk through observed history, the dashed 30/60/90-day forecast, its uncertainty band, safety and physical-limit lines, and the marker for today. Compare OLS and damped-Holt holdout MAE. Point out that the range may be open-ended when the lower slope bound is non-positive.
4. **Discuss evidence and action (30 seconds).** Show detected changes, related events, and the 30-day tenant contribution. State that the leading tenant is a “likely contributor — association only, causation not established.” Read the relevant recommendation as advice only; no action is run by the app.
5. **Spike versus trend (30 seconds).** Search for `NET-R3-01`. Explain that its two-day bandwidth spike resolves and is filtered from the fitting series rather than treated as sustained growth. Contrast with a detected regime change such as Globex onboarding or the later workload changes.
6. **Show audit and tenant view (20 seconds).** Open audit history and show the timestamp, model/version, input date range and fingerprint. Change “Viewing as” to a tenant, note that the displayed metrics and events are recalculated from that tenant's own telemetry, and repeat that this selector is only a demo simulation—not authentication.
7. **Show the mobile notification (30 seconds).** Before the demo, configure a private random ntfy topic on the phone and verify delivery with `python -m backend.notify --test`. Run `python -m backend.notify --simulate-escalation` to replay an earlier synthetic state and the latest forecast in dry-run mode. Show the labelled `[SYNTHETIC] [DEMO]` escalation output (or the received phone message when using the live test), then open the dashboard link and the Notifications page/history. Do not expose a real topic publicly during screen sharing.

**Close:** Backtest results are measured on this synthetic sample with few events. They are not claims of production forecast accuracy or outage reduction.
